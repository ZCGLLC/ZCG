Case Study · eHealth · Insurance Agency

Building a Scalable Medicare Acquisition Engine

How Zaidi Consulting Group helped eHealth strengthen and scale Medicare customer acquisition across traffic, conversion, and qualified call delivery.

Client

eHealth
Insurance Agency

Challenge

eHealth needed to expand Medicare customer acquisition without relying solely on higher spend or additional lead volume. Scaling effectively required a connected strategy that could generate consumer traffic, improve digital conversion, and consistently deliver qualified opportunities to the sales team.

The objective was clear: turn marketing activity into measurable sales opportunities—not simply more traffic.

ZCG Approach

ZCG approached the engagement as more than a call-generation program. We combined performance marketing, proprietary digital assets, sales-funnel optimization, website and advertising optimization, and qualified call delivery to support eHealth across the acquisition lifecycle.

The approach centered on three connected layers: traffic generation, conversion optimization, and qualified call delivery.

Driving Demand Through Proprietary Marketing Channels

ZCG used proprietary marketing websites and digital properties to generate Medicare-related consumer traffic and route prospects into purpose-built conversion funnels—creating an owned source of qualified demand beyond traditional third-party acquisition.

Scaling Performance Advertising

Performance advertising expanded reach into those channels. Campaigns were monitored continuously so stronger-performing opportunities could be scaled while conversion performance remained the priority.

Optimizing The Digital Experience

ZCG also reviewed eHealth’s website and conversion path—messaging, user flow, and conversion points—to reduce friction and strengthen the journey from visitor to customer.

Delivering Qualified Medicare Calls

In parallel, ZCG provided a consistent supply of pre-qualified Medicare calls, giving sales teams a dependable pipeline of consumers who had already demonstrated interest.

Execution

ZCG managed the engagement across the acquisition lifecycle rather than treating each activity as a separate service.

Traffic was generated through proprietary properties and paid campaigns, consumers moved through optimized funnels, performance was analyzed for improvement opportunities, and qualified prospects were delivered to eHealth’s sales organization.

This integrated model allowed ZCG to continually refine the relationship between marketing spend, consumer engagement, call quality, and sales opportunities—improving both acquisition volume and the marketing infrastructure supporting conversion.

Results

The partnership created a scalable acquisition framework that combined owned marketing channels, performance advertising, funnel and website optimization, and qualified call delivery.

Key Outcomes

  • Expanded Medicare consumer traffic through proprietary properties and advertising
  • Improved website and funnel performance to strengthen conversion opportunities
  • Consistent delivery of pre-qualified Medicare calls
  • Campaign optimization based on performance data and conversion behavior
  • A more connected path from digital demand to sales-ready opportunities

Most importantly, ZCG was not simply a call supplier. The engagement helped eHealth improve both acquisition volume and the marketing infrastructure supporting conversion—creating a more cohesive system from first consumer interaction through the sales opportunity.
