Case Study · Connie Health · Medicare

Driving Medicare Growth Through Strategic Marketing & Qualified Call Acquisition

How Zaidi Consulting Group helped Connie Health build a more efficient, scalable Medicare acquisition strategy.

Client

Connie Health
Medicare Health Insurance Provider

The Challenge

When Connie Health approached Zaidi Consulting Group (ZCG), they were looking for additional support to strengthen their Medicare marketing efforts and identify opportunities to improve customer acquisition and revenue performance.

Rather than taking a one-dimensional approach focused solely on lead generation, ZCG took a comprehensive look at Connie Health’s existing marketing efforts. Our team evaluated the current strategy, analyzed performance data, identified areas of opportunity, and developed solutions designed to improve both the quality and consistency of customer acquisition.

The goal was simple: create a more efficient and scalable Medicare acquisition strategy that could consistently generate high-quality opportunities while supporting revenue growth.

Our Approach

ZCG took a hands-on approach to the engagement, combining marketing strategy, analytics, performance analysis, and qualified call acquisition.

1. Marketing & Performance Audit

We began by reviewing Connie Health’s existing marketing efforts and analyzing available performance data. This allowed us to identify opportunities to improve acquisition efficiency, call quality, and overall marketing performance.

Rather than relying on assumptions, our recommendations were driven by data and performance insights.

2. Strategic Analysis

Following the initial audit, ZCG developed a strategic approach focused on improving the flow and quality of Medicare opportunities.

We analyzed key performance indicators, identified potential bottlenecks, and developed actionable solutions designed to create a more predictable customer acquisition process.

3. Implementation

ZCG didn’t stop at recommendations.

Our team worked alongside Connie Health to implement the solutions identified during the strategic analysis. This included establishing a consistent flow of pre-qualified Medicare calls and continuously monitoring performance to help maintain quality and volume.

4. Consistent, High-Quality Call Volume

A critical component of the engagement was providing Connie Health with a reliable source of pre-qualified Medicare calls.

ZCG maintained consistent call volume while focusing on call quality and operational reliability. This gave Connie Health a more predictable pipeline of Medicare prospects and allowed their team to focus on converting qualified opportunities into customers.

The Results

The engagement produced a positive impact on Connie Health’s marketing performance and revenue generation.

By combining strategic marketing analysis, data-driven decision-making, implementation, and consistent qualified call volume, ZCG helped create a more effective and scalable Medicare acquisition strategy.

The results demonstrated the value of taking a comprehensive approach to marketing—not simply generating leads, but analyzing the entire acquisition process and implementing solutions that support measurable business growth.

Key Outcomes

  • Consistent Medicare call volume to support ongoing customer acquisition
  • Pre-qualified prospects delivered directly to the sales organization
  • Improved marketing strategy based on performance analytics and data
  • Hands-on implementation of recommended solutions
  • Positive impact on revenue growth
  • Scalable acquisition infrastructure capable of supporting continued growth
  • Ongoing quality and volume management to maintain a reliable marketing pipeline

The ZCG Difference

What differentiated ZCG was our ability to operate beyond the traditional role of a marketing vendor.

We didn’t simply provide calls and walk away.

We analyzed. We identified opportunities. We developed the strategy. We implemented the solutions. And we measured the results.

By combining consulting expertise with performance marketing and qualified customer acquisition, ZCG was able to help Connie Health strengthen its Medicare marketing efforts and build a more consistent path toward revenue growth.

Conclusion

The partnership with Connie Health demonstrated what happens when strategy, analytics, execution, and performance marketing work together.

ZCG helped transform marketing challenges into actionable solutions while providing the consistent volume and quality needed to support growth.

For organizations looking to scale customer acquisition, improve marketing performance, or build a more predictable revenue pipeline, ZCG brings a strategy-first, results-driven approach designed around the unique needs of each business.
