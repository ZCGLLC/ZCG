Service Overview

From Traffic To Qualified Opportunity.

Zaidi Consulting Group helps healthcare and insurance organizations build performance marketing systems that connect demand generation to sales-ready conversations—not vanity metrics.

We combine proprietary marketing channels, performance advertising, landing pages and funnels, website optimization, and high-intent qualified call delivery. The goal is a clear path: Traffic → Landing Page → Funnel → Qualified Consumer → Call → Conversion.

Whether you need Medicare or ACA customer acquisition support, custom vertical campaigns, or conversion improvements across your digital experience, we help turn marketing spend into measurable opportunities.
