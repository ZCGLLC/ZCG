Our story

Who We Are

Zaidi Consulting Group is a healthcare and business consulting firm committed to helping organizations navigate growth through strategic advisory, operational excellence, and innovative commercial solutions. We work alongside healthcare providers, insurance organizations, and businesses to optimize performance, strengthen operations, and unlock new opportunities for sustainable growth.

Established in 2022, the firm was founded with a simple mission: to solve complex operational challenges for healthcare organizations through practical, results-driven consulting. As our experience and client portfolio expanded, so did our capabilities—evolving into a trusted partner providing business strategy, performance marketing, remote workforce solutions, and commercial growth initiatives.

Since our inception, Zaidi Consulting Group has cultivated strategic relationships with organizations ranging from emerging businesses to nationally recognized healthcare and insurance enterprises. Through these partnerships, we have supported large-scale initiatives, developed scalable growth strategies, and delivered solutions that create measurable business value. Our reputation is built on integrity, collaboration, and an unwavering commitment to delivering results.

At Zaidi Consulting Group, we believe successful partnerships are founded on trust, innovation, and execution. Our mission is to empower organizations with the expertise, strategy, and operational support they need to achieve sustainable, long-term success.
