Our Story

Who We Are

Zaidi Consulting Group is a healthcare growth and business consulting partner. We help healthcare providers, insurance organizations, agencies, and businesses acquire customers, improve revenue performance, and build operations that scale.

Established in 2022, ZCG combines healthcare expertise with performance marketing, qualified customer acquisition, and operational execution. We do not stop at recommendations—we help design, implement, and manage the systems that turn marketing activity into measurable sales opportunities.

From emerging organizations to nationally recognized healthcare and insurance partners, we work alongside client teams to strengthen acquisition, improve conversion, and expand capacity. Based in Texas and serving clients nationally, we build long-term partnerships grounded in clarity, accountability, and results.
